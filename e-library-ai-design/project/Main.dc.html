Developer notes

Tokens
Eight colors above, defined once as CSS variables or in the Tailwind theme. No other colors in UI chrome. Covers use their own muted fills.
Layout
Content max width 1200px, 32px page gutters (16px on mobile), 64px between major sections. Rail is 80px wide on desktop and becomes a bottom tab bar under 768px.
Shadows
Only on book covers (0 4 12 at 20% ink) and overlays: menus and the AI drawer (0 8 32 at 20% ink). Panels and cards are flat with a 1px line border.
Accent use
accent fills the active rail item, progress bars and counters. Any accent-colored text or link uses accent-text to meet AA contrast.
States
Every control has default, hover, focus (2px ink ring), active and disabled. Forms validate inline on blur, show a loading state on submit, and never show raw errors.
Motion
150ms ease for hover and focus, 200ms for the drawer and bottom sheet. Honor prefers-reduced-motion by removing transitions and the skeleton pulse.
Accessibility
WCAG AA text contrast, 44px touch targets, labels above inputs, descriptive cover alt text ("Cover of Frankenstein by Mary Shelley"), icon-only controls carry an aria-label and a tooltip.
Icons
Lucide line icons, 20px, 1.5 stroke. 16px inside buttons and helper text.
