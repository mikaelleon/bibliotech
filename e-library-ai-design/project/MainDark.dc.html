Developer notes: theming

Switching
Define the eight tokens once for light and once for dark. Web: a data-theme attribute or the prefers-color-scheme media query. Android: a second color scheme. Components never hard-code a color.
New token
on-primary is the text color on primary buttons: white in light, #14161D in dark. Needed because primary inverts.
Contrast
ink on bg about 14:1, ink-muted on surface about 6.5:1, accent-text on bg about 7:1, on-primary on primary about 13:1. All pass WCAG AA.
Accent
accent fill is unchanged. Its icon stays white. Accent used as text switches to the lighter accent-text.
Covers
Placeholder covers keep their fills. Real covers get the same 1px cream ring and a 4px radius.
Motion
Same as light: 150ms hover and focus, 200ms drawer and sheet, no motion under reduced-motion.
