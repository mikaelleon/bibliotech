D1 First lines

Each book greets you with its own opening sentence, set huge in the book face. The shelf is the selector. The page is literature before it is marketing.

D2 The shelf answers

The catalog is the hero. Typing lifts matching spines and lowers the rest. Proportion and behaviour, with almost no copy.

D3 Card catalog

A drawer of index cards: author, title, ruled lines, a coral margin. The library's own object. Reads as an illustration at rest.
